Sponsor & Supporter Terms

Last updated: August 2026

1. Visibility

Your text and URL go live 24 hours after your BTC payment is confirmed to the address delivered to you via email. For the Sponsor tier, this also includes your logo and, if requested, an image shown on the Sponsor page only. Your sponsorship or support is shown on both the mainnet (solo100.org) and testnet (test.solo100.org) instances of the pool.

2. Changes

Your text and URL — and, for the Sponsor tier, your logo and image — can each be changed up to 2 times during the paid period. Requested changes take effect within 1 week.

3. Right to Reject

solo100.org reserves the right to reject any submitted text, URL, logo, or image, without explanation.

4. Right to Remove

solo100.org reserves the right to remove a sponsor or supporter listing without refund if the text, URL, logo, or image is changed to illegal content or used in bad faith.

5. Contact

For questions regarding these terms, email bitcoin@solo100.org.
